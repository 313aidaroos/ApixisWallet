# Commerce policy — Ixis only

Apixis sells **one thing for dollars: Ixis**.

Ixis is the native unit on every apple. 100 Ixis = $1. Closed loop tonight.
A future chain token may use the same name. That is a later legal product, not a button on this wallet.

Every product, plan, usage minute, theme, and upgrade is a **redeem**.
No Apixis app may open its own Stripe Checkout for a plan.

## Allowed

- Wallet `/` Ixis packs → Stripe Checkout
- App screens that say **Redeem · 15,000 Ixis** and call Wallet APIs

## Forbidden

- Sister sites selling their own plans on a card
- Marketing Ixis as cash, yield, or an investment
- A live Ixis → chain convert before counsel signs off
- A second coin name on the customer screen (no XP, no APX next to Ixis)

## Peg

100 Ixis = $1. Do not change the peg to chase a token price.
